Surrogate pipeline paper · study part

Study Plan: experiments E0 to E11

Twelve experiments that test, correct and extend the surrogate study in Poznański (2026), M.S. thesis, University of Kansas, Sec. 14.3 and Appendix G. Every pipeline is scored by what it delivers to the design, not only by how well it fits.

Primary end-point regret = f(x̂) − f*, x̂ = optimizer( surrogate( design within budget B ) ) Measured on the true function at matched simulation cost. RMSE, derivative error, rank fidelity on near-optimal regions and calibration are secondary metrics.

Rules for every run

  1. Synthetic problems are scored against the noise-free truth on at least 10,000 Sobol' test points. Noisy cross-validation is shown only as the practitioner's view.
  2. 30 seeds with common random numbers: each seed gives every pipeline the same initial design, noise draw, test set and optimizer starts.
  3. Every learner gets the same tuning budget by nested cross-validation, and its tuning cost is reported.
  4. Simple baselines are always present: linear and quadratic response surfaces, inverse-distance weighting, k-nearest neighbours, plus random and Sobol' or maximin designs.
  5. Hypotheses H1 to H8 are frozen before E3 starts. Any deviation is logged.
  6. Each run is one configuration row with a content hash and a result file, so jobs can be stopped, resumed and split across machines.

Strengths, weaknesses and blind problems

Every method has at least one problem built to favour it and one built to hurt it, with the mechanism stated in advance. A blind set has no designed winner, so the results also show what wins where nobody predicted. A checker script enforces this; the full matrix is in docs/PROBLEM_MATRIX.md.

Experiments

E0

Infrastructure and port check

Problem library with gradients, known optima, fidelity variants and noise wrappers; learner registry; samplers; multi-fidelity wrappers; upscalers; optimizer harness; resumable runner; statistics and plots.

Gate: reproduce Appendix G Tables canonical_a/b and scout_knee with the original settings, within their confidence intervals, before any new result.

E1

Thesis errata

Re-run Appendix G with the fixes and publish both versions side by side as a paper appendix.

B-01 clean truthB-02 Sobol' controlB-05 nested target choiceB-06 noise estimatorB-08 30 seedsB-11 CIs on real cacheB-12 matched settings

Decision: does the GP still win every synthetic surface once the 3 % noise floor is removed?

E2

Screening

Reference pipeline: Sobol' start, GP-variance scout, GP intermediate, 24-point cubic grid, IPOPT multistart. Swap one stage at a time over its full menu on 12 problems, 3 budgets, 2 noise levels, 10 seeds.

  • Keep the top five per stage, every thesis method, and any method that wins on at least one problem class.
  • Measured timings replace the compute estimates below.
E3

Learner study

RQ1H1

About 12 surviving learners across all synthetic blocks, d up to 10, n from 5d to 300d, five noise levels and five noise types, 30 seeds.

  • Learning curves and crossover maps showing where gradient-boosted trees overtake the GP.
  • Mixed-effects model of error against budget, noise, dimension and landscape features.
E4

Why trees won on the FEA data

H3

On the feature probes, the FEA cache and the aircraft data: smooth the training target at increasing length-scales, rotate the inputs randomly, add dummy inputs, check GP hyperparameters hitting their bounds, and test residuals for heavy tails.

E5

Scout × deliverable

RQ2H2

Full factorial of 16 scouts (including a frozen-hyperparameter GP, model-free sequential maximin, random and Sobol') against 7 deliverables, plus retrospective active learning on the FEA cache.

  • Native pairings (scout and deliverable from the same family) reported separately from cross pairings.
  • Samples needed to reach a target, measured against a Sobol' design, not only random sampling.
E6

Multi-fidelity

RQ3H4

Bridge functions, AR1, recursive and hierarchical co-kriging, NARGP, multi-fidelity deep GP and neural nets, feature-augmented trees and nets, and space mapping. Fidelity correlation 0.5–0.99, linear or nonlinear relation, cost ratio 3–1000, low-fidelity budget share 0–80 %, all at matched cost.

  • Error grids per learner family and maps of where multi-fidelity pays.
  • On the FEA cache: does the thesis TMF target already capture the gain from t_scalar?
E7

Change of basis and co-learning

H8

Projection pursuit, active-subspace GP, polynomial ridge, warped GP, deep kernel learning in four training variants, static SINDy autoencoder, compressed sensing in Fourier, Chebyshev, Legendre and wavelet bases, functional SVD, tensor-train cross, and automatic co-search.

  • Each feature probe is paired with the basis it favours and the bases it does not.
  • Learned encoders are also tested on the large datasets (10³–10⁵ points), where they should work.
  • Compression sub-study: randomized SVD and tensor-train on the upscaling grid, Nyström scouts, speed against error up to d = 10.
E8

Upscaling

RQ4H5

Grid sizes 6–48 per axis with four interpolants, multilevel B-splines, THB-splines, direct P-splines, Smolyak sparse grids, Chebyshev, network distillation and compressed grids, applied to smooth and rough intermediate models.

Key question: can a smoothing upscaler turn gradient-boosted trees into a good surrogate for a gradient-based optimizer?

E9

Optimization on the surrogate

RQ5RQ6H6H7

Surrogates from E3 and E8 against 11 optimizers (IPOPT, SLSQP, L-BFGS-B, trust-constr, Nelder–Mead, COBYLA, DIRECT, DE, CMA-ES, PSO, basin hopping), plus loops that query the true function (EGO, LCB, TuRBO, random-forest EI, DYCORS, trust-region model management).

  • Does the RMSE ranking predict the regret ranking, per optimizer class?
  • At what simulation cost per run does each level of sophistication pay off?
E10

Selection model

RQ7

Predict the best pipeline from features of the initial design only: landscape features, fill distance, estimated smoothness and anisotropy, noise estimate, fidelity correlation, d, n and cost. Validated leaving whole problems and whole problem classes out, then blind on the real data.

E11

Real-data transfer

RQ8

FEA cache and aircraft data: 30 repeats of nested cross-validation, retrospective active learning, multi-fidelity with t_scalar, the E4 diagnostics, and an optimization proxy that ranks the chosen design among held-out runs.
