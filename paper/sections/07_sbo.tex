\section{Surrogate-based optimization and the accuracy fallacy}
\label{sec:sbo}

\paragraph{Optimizers.} Once a surrogate exists, any optimizer can be run on it: gradient-based interior-point and SQP
methods \cite{wachter2006implementation,nocedal2006numerical,kraft1988software}; derivative-free local methods
\cite{nelder1965simplex,powell1994direct,audet2006mesh}; deterministic global search \cite{jones1993lipschitzian}; and
stochastic global methods \cite{storn1997differential,hansen2001completely,kennedy1995particle,wales1997global}.
Surrogate-managed methods instead return to the true function: EGO \cite{jones1998efficient}, trust-region model
management with provable convergence \cite{alexandrov1998trust}, SNOBFIT \cite{huyer2008snobfit}, stochastic-RBF methods
\cite{regis2007stochastic,regis2013combining}, SMAC and TPE \cite{hutter2011sequential,bergstra2011algorithms}, and TuRBO
\cite{eriksson2019scalable}. Surrogate-assisted evolutionary algorithms form a parallel literature
\cite{jin2005comprehensive,jin2011surrogate}.

\paragraph{Accuracy is not the objective.} Several independent results show that the most accurate surrogate is not
reliably the one that produces the best decision. Eggensperger et al.\ found gradient boosting gave the lowest RMSE while
random forests gave the highest rank correlation on all nine datasets, and that tree surrogates reproduced optimizer
behaviour where GP surrogates sometimes did not \cite{eggensperger2015efficient,eggensperger2018efficient}. Williams and
Cremaschi found MARS and GP best for approximation but random forests, SVR and GP most robust for surrogate-based
optimization \cite{williams2021selection}. Loshchilov et al.\ use a surrogate that only ranks candidates, because
comparison-based evolution strategies need nothing else \cite{loshchilov2012self}. Neufang et al.\ observed optimizer
rankings change between test functions and chemical-process cases \cite{neufang2024surrogate}, and Lütjens et al.\
found the ranking of climate emulators flipped with the noise level of the training data \cite{lutjens2024climatebench}.
Theory explains why (Section~\ref{sec:theory}): the regret of the surrogate's optimum is bounded by the surrogate error on
the near-optimal sublevel sets, not by its global mean-squared error, and gradient-based optimizers additionally depend
on derivative accuracy.

\paragraph{Benchmarks.} Test-function libraries---the Surjanovic--Bingham virtual library used here, the 175-function
survey of Jamil and Yang, and the COCO/BBOB platform \cite{jamil2013literature,hansen2021coco}---classify functions by
shape, modality, separability and conditioning; exploratory landscape analysis computes such features from samples
\cite{mersmann2011exploratory,kerschke2019comprehensive} and supports automated algorithm selection. Benchmarking
practice---performance and data profiles, multiple-problem statistics and their Bayesian alternatives
\cite{dolan2002benchmarking,more2009benchmarking,demsar2006statistical,benavoli2017time,bartzbeielstein2020benchmarking}---and
surrogate-based benchmark suites \cite{pfisterer2022yahpo} complete the toolkit.
