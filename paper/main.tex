\begin{abstract}
\textit{Draft.} Surrogate models stand in for expensive simulations inside design loops. Engineering practice is dominated by Gaussian-process (kriging) surrogates, while tabular machine learning and several simulation-heavy sciences are dominated by tree ensembles; a thesis finite-element study found gradient-boosted trees out-predicting every Gaussian-process variant on real data while losing on smooth synthetic surfaces. This paper reviews surrogate, uncertainty, sampling, multi-fidelity, change-of-basis, upscaling and surrogate-based-optimization methods across disciplines, from computation theory to engineering practice, and then (in the study part, in preparation) scores complete pipelines by the true-function regret they deliver per unit of simulation cost.
\end{abstract}
